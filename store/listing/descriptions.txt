FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------
CRIST Tool is the official mobile app for authorised field collectors in the UsmleWise CRIST developmental screening study.

Built for reliable data collection in the field, CRIST Tool helps trained collectors register participants, complete screening questionnaires, and sync results securely — even when connectivity is limited.

KEY FEATURES

• Secure collector sign-in with phone and password
• Geo-tagged submissions for verified field activity
• Works offline — records queue locally and sync automatically when back online
• Dynamic screening questionnaires managed by study administrators
• Verbal caregiver consent recorded on every submission
• Optional medical-record photo capture
• Collector payment tracking with transparent due amounts
• English, Hindi, and Kannada language support
• Profile management and account deletion

WHO IS THIS FOR?

CRIST Tool is intended for authorised research collectors participating in the CRIST study. It is not a public consumer app and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

REQUIREMENTS

• Authorised collector account
• Location enabled while using the app (submissions are geo-tagged)
• Verbal consent from a caregiver before collecting participant data
